♧ First burn

The first burn sets the candle's memory. Light the candle and allow the wax to melt to the full diameter of the vessel before extinguishing — usually 2–3 hours for our standard pillars, longer for larger sculptural forms. Skipping this step creates a tunnel through the centre that the candle will follow for the rest of its life.

✂ Trim the wick

Trim the wick to about 5 mm before every burn. A trimmed wick burns clean, with a stable flame and almost no soot. Long or curled wicks flicker, smoke, and shorten the life of the candle.

▱ Storage

Keep candles in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight. South African sun can fade pigment and soften the wax. Store sculptural pieces upright on a flat surface; cover with the dust cover if travelling.

♢ Safety

Never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep candles out of reach of children and pets, and away from curtains and other flammable surfaces. Always burn on a heat-safe surface. Stop burning when about 10 mm of wax remains in the vessel.
