The details.

Materials & craft

Each candle is carefully hand-poured in small batches. Made with a natural wax blend and a cotton wick. Small variations in colour, texture and finish make every candle unique.

Burn & care

Trim the wick before lighting. Place your candle on a heat-resistant surface and keep it away from drafts. Never leave a burning candle unattended.

Delivery & collection

Hand-finished to order and dispatched within 1-2 business days. Couriered nationwide through The Courier Guy. Delivery then takes 1-2 business days in Rustenburg and 3-5 elsewhere in South Africa. Free delivery on orders over R 550; below that, R 60 to a Courier Guy locker or R 90 to your door. Or collect free from us in Foxlake in Rustenburg.

Returns

Unused pieces can be returned within 14 days of delivery. You pay the courier to send a return back, unless it arrived damaged or faulty. Custom and personalised pieces are non-refundable unless faulty.
